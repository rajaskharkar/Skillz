Scyra investor and stakeholder product demo

Communication job
By the end, investors and stakeholders should understand Scyra as a cohesive system for intentional work, reflection, planning, and long-term return—not a collection of disconnected productivity tools—and should be ready to support or demo it because the deck shows the complete product loop and its retention layers in the latest Android build.

Primary source
- Scyra Android app, package com.kingkharnivore.skillz.scyra, locally built and captured on an Android emulator on 2026-08-19.
- Screenshots are stored in .codex-build/scyra-investor/assets.
- A reversible emulator-only demo snapshot was used to expose populated collection, creature, badge, currency, and reward states. No production app code was changed for those screenshots.

Feature inventory represented in the deck
- Story: journey filters, Day/Week/Month views, period navigation, time and score rollups, Sagas/Chronicles, session cards.
- Flow: scored and Soft modes, live timer, journey tagging, Surge targets, background runtime, Pulse capture during a session, Arc continuation, completion.
- Chronicle and Pulse: text, dictation/audio, gallery, camera photo, video, journey tagging, attachment to an active Flow.
- Rewards: Scyra Points, Pearls, reward carousel, creature and Arc outcomes.
- Paths: planned Flows, planned Arcs, five-step Studio, suggested Scenes, route previews, save or begin an Arc.
- ScratchPad: rich-text editing, heading and font styles, emphasis, search, undo/redo, navigation.
- Settings/help: score visibility, Calm Mode, app languages, Health Connect, movement bonus, feature education.
- The Shell: Pearl economy, hub, notifications, objectives, stats/records, idea capture, focus exercises, Stillwater, The Blue, Chest, Badges.
- The Lookout: daily/weekly/monthly objectives and Pearl rewards.
- Voyage Hall: totals, records, streaks, core and bonus progress.
- Idea Grove: Pulse-derived ideas, alive/completed states, launch a Flow from an idea.
- Focus Room: guided mindfulness exercises with voice support.
- Stillwater: Soft Flow Drops, vessels, exclusive creature collection.
- The Blue: depth zones, encounters, creature growth with Pearls, release, collection progress.
- Chest: inventory, creature details, levels, sorting/filtering, grow/place/release actions.
- Badges: progress, pinning, tracking, showcase, collections, mastery celebration.

Deck source-note convention
Every slide contains a [Sources] block in speaker notes. All visible product claims are grounded in the locally captured application and repository inspection; the deck makes no external market-size, traction, or financial claims.
